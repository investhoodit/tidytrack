Tidy Track System Code of Conduct

1. Treat all team members, mentors, and stakeholders with respect and courtesy

  • Behaviour: Offensive, discriminatory, or unprofessional behavior in chats, calls, or documents is strictly prohibited.
  • Collaboration sessions: (video/audio) sessions must maintain professionalism—dress modestly.
  • Avoid: disruptive backgrounds/noise, and use respectful language.

2. Work Hours & Availability

  • All staff are expected to be online and available during official working hours (08:00 – 17:00, unless otherwise agreed).
  • Status indicators ('Available,' 'Away,' etc.) must accurately reflect your availability.
  • Extended absences (more than 30 minutes) must be communicated in advance to supervisors via the app.
  • Monitoring and analyzing usage and trends to improve our performance.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share your information with:

  • Service Providers: We may use third-party companies to perform services on our behalf, such as hosting, analytics, and payment processing.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

4. Task & Project Management

  • Each member must regularly update task progress, deliverables, and timelines in the system.
  • Deadlines must be respected; if challenges arise, escalate early through the app’s reporting tools.
  • Only assign or reassign tasks with team lead/manager approval.

5. Performance & Accountability

Performance tracking within the app is transparent.

Metrics such as deadlines met, contributions, and meeting participation are monitored.

Staff are responsible for ensuring their own deliverables are completed to the best standard.

Plagiarism, falsifying progress, or misrepresenting work is considered misconduct.

6. Collaboration & Communication

Use the built-in chat and video conferencing for official collaboration only; refrain from unrelated personal discussions during work hours.

Always mute your microphone when not speaking in calls, and respect structured agendas.

Share files and documents responsibly; ensure version control and clear labeling.

7. Remote Work Etiquette

Maintain a reliable internet connection and functional equipment (laptop, headphones, webcam).

Participate actively in all scheduled virtual meetings unless excused.

Backgrounds (physical or virtual) should be professional and not distracting.

8. Confidentiality & Data Security

Project data, client information, and internal communications are strictly confidential.

Do not share access credentials or project files outside authorized channels.

Follow IT security policies: use strong passwords, lock your screen when away, and avoid public Wi-Fi without VPN.

9. Learning & Mentorship

As a WIL program, interns and graduates must actively seek feedback and participate in mentorship sessions.

Respect mentors’ time and guidance by preparing questions and applying feedback.

Document learning outcomes in the system where applicable.

10. Consequences of Misconduct

Breaches of this code may result in corrective action, including warnings, suspension from projects, or termination of internship/employment.

Serious breaches (e.g., misconduct, data leaks, harassment) will be escalated to HR and management immediately.

11. Acknowledgement

All staff must acknowledge and accept this Code of Conduct upon first login.

Continued use of the system implies consent to abide by its rules.
